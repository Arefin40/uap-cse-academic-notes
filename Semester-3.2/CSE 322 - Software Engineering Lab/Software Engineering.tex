\section{Objectives \& Project output}
This project focuses on developing an innovative online marketplace with social media features to support Bangladesh's small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). By combining e-commerce with social interaction, the platform offers an engaging experience. Users can browse a home page similar to a social media newsfeed showcasing shops and their products. Users can easily explore shop profiles, view detailed product information, and purchase products directly through the platform.

Our goal is to empower SMBs by providing them with a dedicated platform to showcase their businesses effectively, overcoming the challenges of competing in larger marketplaces. This initiative aims to enhance product visibility and foster growth for local enterprises in the digital era.
