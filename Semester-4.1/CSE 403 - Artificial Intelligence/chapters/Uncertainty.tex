\subsection*{Inference using Joint Distributions}

For the following joint distribution, find the conditional probability of
$P(cavity | \neg toothache)$. Indicate query variable, evidence variable and hidden variable. Where the values of joint distribution are $A = 0.324$, $B=1-A$, $C = 0.576$ and $D = 0.144$.

\begin{center}
   \begin{tabularx}{0.5\textwidth}{|C|C|C|}\hline
      & \multicolumn{2}{|c|}{$\neg$ toothache}
      \\\hline
      & catch & $\neg$ catch
      \\\hline
      cavity & 0.324 & 0.676
      \\\hline
      $\neg$ cavity & 0.576 & 0.144
      \\\hline
   \end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\textbf{Solution:}\\[0.5em]

$\begin{aligned}
   P(cavity | \neg toothache) &= \frac{P(cavity \cap \neg toothache)}{P(\neg toothache)} = \frac{0.324+0.676}{0.324+0.676+0.576+0.144} = \frac{1}{1.72}
\end{aligned}$
