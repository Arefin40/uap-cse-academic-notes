\subsection*{Examples}
\textbf{Question-1:} Represent the following knowledge statements using propositional logic. Apply rules of inference to conclude \textbf{"The laptop is working"}.
\begin{enumerate}
   \item Either the laptop is working or I will study at the library.
   \item If I study at the library, then I will not attend the online seminar.
   \item I attended the online seminar.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Solution:}\\[1em]
Let propositions are,\\
\begin{tabular}{ll}
   $P$ &: The laptop is working. \\
   $Q$ &: I will study at the library. \\
   $R$ &: I will attend the online seminar. \\
\end{tabular}

Given Statements,\\
\begin{tabular}{ll}
   $P \lor Q$ & $\ldots (1)$\\
   $Q \to \neg R$ & $\ldots (2)$\\
   $R$ & $\ldots (3)$\\
\end{tabular}


From statement (3) and (2), by Modus Tollens, we get,\\
$\begin{aligned}
   & Q \to \neg R \\
   & R \\\hline
   & \therefore \neg Q \ldots\ldots (4) \\
\end{aligned}$

From statement (1) and (4), by Disjunctive Syllogism, we get,\\
$\begin{aligned}
   & P \lor Q \\
   & \neg Q \\\hline
   & \therefore P \\
\end{aligned}$


\textbf{Question-2:} Represent the following knowledge statements using propositional logic. Apply rules of inference to conclude \textbf{"Applications can be processed"}.
\vspace{-\baselineskip}
\begin{enumerate}
   \item If Admission office is open, Staff are present
   \item If Staff are present, Applications can be processed
   \item If emergency drill is active, the building is evacuated
   \item The emergency drill is not active
   \item The admission office is open or the building is evacuated.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Solution:}\\[1em]
Let propositions are,\\
\begin{tabular}{ll}
   $P$ &: Admission office is open. \\
   $Q$ &: Staff are present. \\
   $R$ &: Applications can be processed. \\
   $S$ &: Emergency drill is active. \\
   $T$ &: Building is evacuated. \\
\end{tabular}

Given Statements,\\
\begin{tabular}{ll}
   $P \to Q$ & $\ldots (1)$\\
   $Q \to R$ & $\ldots (2)$\\
   $S \to T$ & $\ldots (3)$\\
   $\neg S$ & $\ldots (4)$\\
   $P \lor T$ & $\ldots (5)$\\
\end{tabular}


From statement (4) and (3), by Modus Tollens, we get,\\
$\begin{aligned}
   & S \to T \\
   & \neg S \\\hline
   & \therefore \neg T \ldots\ldots (6) \\
\end{aligned}$

From statement (5) and (6), by Disjunctive Syllogism, we get,\\
$\begin{aligned}
   & P \lor T \\
   & \neg T \\\hline
   & \therefore P \\
\end{aligned}$

From statement (1) and (7), by Modus Ponens, we get,\\
$\begin{aligned}
   & P \to Q \\
   & P \\\hline
   & \therefore Q \\
\end{aligned}$

From statement (2) and (8), by Modus Ponens, we get,\\
$\begin{aligned}
   & Q \to R \\
   & Q \\\hline
   & \therefore R \\
\end{aligned}$

Thus, we conclude that \textbf{"Applications can be processed"}.
